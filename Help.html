If You Are Being Bullied

If you are being bullied, remember that you are not alone and it is never okay. Speaking to someone you trust, such as a parent, teacher, or friend, is an important first step. You deserve to feel safe and supported.

If You See Someone Being Bullied

If you see someone being bullied, don't ignore it. If it is safe to do so, support the person being bullied and let a trusted adult know what happened. Even small acts of kindness can make a big difference.

Need Someone to Talk To?

If you or someone you know needs help, we're here to listen. Click the button below to contact us and access our confidential helpline. You don't have to face bullying alone.
